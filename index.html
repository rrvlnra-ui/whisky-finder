      About these ratings, medals and prices

      Critic score is the expert tasting score out of 100 published on Distiller, with the reviewer named. Community is Distiller's average user rating out of 5, with the number of ratings. Bottles not listed on Distiller show no scores.

      Medals come from the major whisky competitions, including SFWSC, IWSC, ISC, the World Whiskies Awards, the Australian Distilled Spirits and International Spirits Awards, TWSC, the Irish, Canadian and New Zealand awards, the Spirits Business Masters and Meininger's ISW. Each medal links to the results page it came from. Not every competition publishes a searchable list of ordinary Gold, Silver and Bronze results, so "No medals found" means none were found in these sources, not that the bottle has never won.

      Where to buy lists prices read from retailer websites on the date shown. Prices and stock change often, so confirm on the shop's site before buying.
